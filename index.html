Source & proxy settings

A static page can't read other sites directly, so the URL is fetched through a public CORS proxy. If one fails, try another. Sites needing a login or heavy JavaScript may not import — use “Type it” for those.

Works best on mainstream recipe sites that publish structured data (taste.com.au, RecipeTin Eats, AllRecipes, BBC Good Food, and most others).

These live in this browser and join the swap pool right away. Custom meals price by whatever you enter here — they don't scale with the people count the way bank recipes do.
